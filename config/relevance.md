# What "relevant" means for Debrief

This is read by the triage model (M4), so editing it changes what gets picked; it is versioned
with the prompt. The goal: help me build an intuition for how the AI/ML landscape is changing,
and what changes to expect.

Relevant means it is likely to reshape the field, or it shows how the field is moving. For example:

- **A new tool developers will start using**: something that changes how people build, not a
  minor version or a feature tweak.
- **A new model with unique performance**: a capability, price point or domain that wasn't
  available before (for example, a Gemini Flash built for robotics), open or closed.
- **A discovery about model behavior that reshapes development**: findings that change how
  people train, evaluate, prompt or deploy models.
- **Policy and international news that shows the shape of the landscape**: regulation, export
  controls, national programs, and comparisons between countries or labs (for example, how many
  frontier-model tokens each country generates).
- **Anything else that changes my picture of where AI is going**: big shifts in compute,
  capital, talent, or who leads in what.

Not relevant, however well covered: routine product updates, customer stories, tutorials,
marketing, events and webinars, and incremental results that don't change what people do.

In Alex's words (2026-10-22): "Relevant for me means something that is going to reshape the
field. This can be from a new tool that developers are going to begin using, a new model that
offers unique performance like Jev or even Gemini Flash for robotic, or a discovery about the
model behavior that reshapes development. Even policy and international news (like comparing
frontier tokens generated) helps me understand the landscape. The idea behind relevant is
anything that helps me understand how the AI/ML landscape is changing and developing. The goal is
that this helps me build an intuition for what changes to expect."
